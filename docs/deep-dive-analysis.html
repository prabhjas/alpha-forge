What Happened

crisis CEO Jensen Huang resigned. Board is weighing leadership transition. Stock dropped ~10% on the news (Nov 5), then continued sliding.
selloff Last 10 days: -24%. From $213 on Oct 29 to $173 today. The sharpest single-stock correction in the AI sector this year.
context Jensen was synonymous with Nvidia. He built the company from a gaming GPU maker into the dominant AI chip company. His departure creates real uncertainty about strategic direction.

Bull Case — Why Buy

1. RSI 26 = deeply oversold. Last time NVDA hit RSI below 30, it rallied 40%+ in the next 3 months. This is a mechanical signal, not an opinion.
2. Forward P/E at 15-year low (~11.7x). For a company growing revenue 90%+ YoY, this is historically cheap. Multiple analysts call it "silly cheap."
3. The business hasn't changed. AI chip demand is structural. Blackwell architecture is shipping. Data center revenue is $30B+/quarter. The CEO leaving doesn't erase the moat.
4. Dan Ives (Wedbush) sees 53% upside. Multiple analysts call this "the opportunity of a lifetime" and a "screaming buy."
5. 62% monthly win rate, median +3.8%/month over 5 years. Even with the drawdowns, the long-term compounder story is intact.

Bear Case — Why Wait

1. CEO risk is real. Jensen wasn't just a CEO — he was the visionary. Board hasn't named a successor. This uncertainty could last weeks.
2. Below both 50-day ($217) and 200-day ($204) averages. Technically broken. The trend is down, and catching a falling knife is dangerous.
3. Volatility is 39%. That's 2.5x the S&P 500. A ₹50K position could swing ±₹15K in a month.
4. AMD surging (+133% 12m) while NVDA falls. Market may be rotating to competitors, not just selling the sector.
5. Max drawdown since 2021: -66%. This stock has gone from $346 to $115 before. A 27% drop could become a 40% drop.

My Assessment

Verdict: Small, disciplined buy — not an all-in.
The oversold signal (RSI 26) and valuation (P/E 11.7x) are compelling. But the CEO departure is a genuine structural risk, not just noise. The right move is a starter position — buy a small amount now, and add more if: (a) a credible successor is named, (b) the price holds above $160, or (c) earnings confirm the business is fine without Jensen. Do NOT put more than 15-20% of your risk capital here.
