Can we hedge with calls and puts?

India

  • One Nifty option lot is 65 units ≈ ₹16.3 lakh of exposure. Your India allocation is ₹1.5 lakh. A put that size is a bet 11× bigger than what it protects.
  • Our 2019–2026 tests: buying calls averaged −1.8% per trade (median −10.6%). Buying puts lost on 61% of trades (median −17.6%).
  • SEBI's FY26 study: 87.7% of individual F&O traders lost money.

US

  • INDmoney offers US stocks and ETFs, not US options, and RBI's remittance rules do not allow margin or derivative trading abroad. Please confirm in the app, but treat this as closed.
  • The hedge that fits this size is cash, diversification and exit rules. That is what the plan uses.
The ₹20,000 + ₹20,000 options sandbox: skip it for now. If you want to learn options, I can add an options paper book to the engine and we judge it after eight weeks, with no money at risk.
